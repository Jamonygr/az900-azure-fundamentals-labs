# Study pathways

## First successful deployment

Complete Lab 00, cloud concepts 01–05, then architecture 06 and networking 08. Validate and clean up both live deployments before proceeding. Explain what the subscription, resource group, VNet, and subnet each control.

## Complete exam preparation

Work through 00–20 in order, then the capstone. For each lab: explain the concept in your own words, complete the exercise, record the expected observation, take all 30 questions, and revisit every incorrect answer through its remediation link. Aim for reasoning consistency on a second attempt; a practice score is not an exam-pass prediction.

| Week | Suggested focus |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Readiness and Labs 01–05; responsibility and service-model decisions |
| 2 | Labs 06–10; architecture, compute, networking, storage, migration |
| 3 | Labs 11–16; identity, access, security, cost, governance |
| 4 | Labs 17–21; management, deployment, monitoring, capstone, review |

The schedule is a suggested pace, not a prerequisite. Allow extra time for permissions and cleanup. Durations in the catalog exclude tool installation and role propagation.

## Final review

Use the [objective map](objective-map.md) as a checklist. Describe why each incorrect option is wrong, rather than memorizing its letter. Finish the official [practice assessment](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-fundamentals/) and explore Microsoft's exam sandbox from the certification page.
